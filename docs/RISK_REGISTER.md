# RISK_REGISTER

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Unity/Android toolchain unavailable, so no APK can be produced or measured | Certain in this environment | Blocks the entire client and every release gate | Everything not requiring Unity was completed and tested first; the client boundary is defined by interfaces so the domain engine can be reused unchanged | `BLOCKED_TOOLCHAIN`, open |
| R-02 | A future contributor reintroduces a tier above 1000 km | Medium (it happened in v3.1) | Direct violation of DL-1 | Enum ordering, CHECK constraints, 44 pgTAP tests, static scanner, CI gate | Mitigated |
| R-03 | Launch content is padded with reskins to hit the floor | High without tooling | Violates DL-4 and # 29.2 | Semantic duplicate detection across 8 content kinds with per-kind thresholds; caught a real case during development | Mitigated |
| R-04 | Content data is mistaken for playable content in reporting | High | Dishonest completion claim | `DATA_PASS` versus `PLAYABLE_PASS` separation (ADR-003), applied in every document | Mitigated |
| R-05 | Concurrent session finishes double-count a checkpoint or a streak | Medium | Reward integrity failure | `FOR UPDATE` on the monthly row, unique keys on claims, crown and reward day; idempotency key on the ledger | Mitigated, needs a real concurrent load test |
| R-06 | The JS engine, the C# client and the SQL authority drift apart | Medium | Client preview disagrees with the server's authoritative result | Shared conformance fixture exported from JS, replayed by C# (29 tests) and by SQL (992 assertions); both generators run in CI and fail on stale output | **Closed** |
| R-07 | Health Connect import double-claims a run already captured directly | Medium | Duplicate reward | Unique `(user_id, source_record_id)` and `(user_id, metric_fingerprint)`; import applies through the same idempotent transaction | Mitigated in schema, untested end to end |
| R-08 | Raw GPS route leaks through a view or an analytics event | Low | Serious privacy incident | Route lives in `private`, which has no client grant; pgTAP asserts unreachability; no analytics pipeline exists yet to leak into | Mitigated |
| R-09 | The 4 GB-class 30 fps target proves unreachable once the client exists | Unknown | Would pressure someone to cut continents | DL-4 forbids that resolution; the answer is tiered streaming and content packs | Open, unmeasurable here |
| R-10 | Korean-only content strings ship without native review in other languages | Medium | Poor localization quality | ko and en authored together; other locales are structured but must be flagged `BLOCKED_LOCALIZATION_REVIEW` before shipping | Open |
| R-11 | Trademark or IP conflict on continent, character or product naming | Medium | Store rejection or rename late in development | Names are original and deliberately unlike existing IP; a formal search is a legal task | `BLOCKED_LEGAL_REVIEW`, open |
| R-12 | The session ends before the work is pushed, losing everything | Certain if unhandled | Total loss — the container is ephemeral | Commit and push at every green unit; `HANDOFF.md` records exact resume commands | Mitigated |
